Installing it

  1. Tap Download Précis for Android above, on your Android phone.
  2. Open the file when it finishes. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps. Android asks this once.
  3. Tap Install, then Open, then Join with a code. To get the code: on a PC that already runs Précis, open Settings, then Your devices, then Add a device, and press Make a code. A code lasts 10 minutes and works once.

Already have it?

To update, download it again from this page and tap Update. Your mail and your settings stay as they are.
